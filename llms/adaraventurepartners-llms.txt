## Investment thesis
AI is rewriting how companies build defensibility. Software became cheaper to build; incumbents are more exposed; markets form faster than capital can follow. Advantage has shifted to what generic AI cannot shortcut: proprietary signal, trust, domain depth, and physical systems.

Defensibility is a spectrum, not a formula:
- More directly changed by AI: workflow (agents rewrite it), distribution (rebuilt around agents), ecosystem (lock-ins loosen)
- Depends on combination and context: data (compounding wins), scale (capex still matters), network (trust intensifies)
- More embedded in real-world systems: regulatory (licenses hold), physical (hardest to replicate)

Position on this spectrum reflects exposure to AI, not business quality. The strongest companies combine several advantages and compound them over time.

We look for founders with:
1. Big vision — a market others cannot yet see, built for global scale from day one
2. Execution — talent density and the rate at which a founder learns, not just speed
3. Technical conviction — a precise account of why technical choices create a position others cannot easily replicate
4. Courage — conviction to commit before the narrative becomes consensus

Values in practice: committed, transparent, focused, reliable.

## Four verticals
We see the greatest opportunity in four market layers. Our verticals describe where we look. The founder determines what we back.

- [Capture — New Data Frontiers](https://www.adara.vc/about): Critical industries still run on incomplete, fragmented, or invisible data. The first company to capture proprietary signal can build an intelligence layer others cannot easily copy. Examples: SatVu (thermal earth observation), AdCities (geo-contextual outdoor media intelligence), Fluzo (audio fingerprinting), Zymvol (computational enzyme design), Wirescan (power-cable monitoring).
- [Secure — Trusted AI Infrastructure](https://www.adara.vc/about): AI cannot enter regulated industries without proof it is secure, sovereign and compliant. As AI moves into enterprise workflows, trust becomes a core buying criterion. Examples: Acoru (fraud and scam prevention), TandemTrace (AI agents for SecOps), Wodan AI (fully homomorphic encryption), 42Crunch (API security), Constella Intelligence, CounterCraft, Airflows.
- [Compute — Deep Domain Computation](https://www.adara.vc/about): Enterprises hold decades of operational knowledge that generic AI cannot turn into expert-grade decisions. Domain depth is a defensible layer. Examples: Articul8 (industrial domain-specific GenAI), AppFactor (self-healing enterprise software), Quibim (AI radiology), IOMED (ML for clinical research), TuringDream (multi-agent enterprise apps).
- [Connect — Real-World Systems](https://www.adara.vc/about): Physical systems — grids, factories, infrastructure — remain largely disconnected from intelligent software. Bringing intelligence into the physical world creates advantage that is hard to replicate. Examples: Corinex (broadband over power line), XYZ Reality (engineering-grade AR for construction), Prisma Photonics, Eluviant.

## Why Europe
Europe under-converts its tech edge: world-class research, engineering talent and industrial depth have not produced enough globally scaled technology companies. The next cycle rewards proprietary data, deep domain expertise, trusted deployment and real-world integration — areas where Europe is structurally strong (industry as dataset, efficient talent, regulation as trust, complexity as moat). Adara is built to convert that edge into category-defining companies, with a pan-European scope, a Southern Europe core, and expansion into North America.

## How we work with founders
Twenty years of building companies means we know where founders get stuck. We help make the hire, hold the line in the negotiation, open the door to the customer, and stay in the room for every crucible round.

Four ways we show up:
1. We speak your language — engineers and operators helping turn technology into commercial outcomes
2. Edge in pivotal moments — fundraising, board dynamics, tough rounds, exits
3. Partnerships that unlock value — network spanning global tech platforms and research labs; early customers and co-investors
4. 20-year Europe–US network of operators and buyers

Platform in practice (see [Our approach](https://www.adara.vc/about)):
- Winning new markets — e.g. Articul8 (European/LatAm enterprise revenue), AlienVault (Silicon Valley relocation and syndicate), CounterCraft (US presence)
- Building the team — e.g. Wirescan (leadership rebuild), Eluviant (CRO/CEO and GTM), Plenit (CFO/CRO)
- Backing through the rounds — e.g. Acoru (led seed, assembled Series A), SatVu (follow-on signalling), XYZ Reality (followed on through a decade-long repositioning)
- Exiting the right way — e.g. PlayGiga (board through M&A; acquired by Meta), HDIV (seed to Datadog), Cyber Guru (hold for the larger outcome; exited to Libraesva)

We also run founder-only sessions and partnerships with platforms including NVIDIA, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, AWS, Microsoft and Databricks.
